Welcome Auggie

August Allan Dorn was born on 4/3/21 at 3:51pm weighing 8lb 7oz and was 20 inches tall. The only good thing out of COVID!

After a night at the hospital we brought him home Easter Sunday and are looking forward to spending every moment we can with him. For both him and Dewey there are lots of changes in the coming weeks.

Elyse was incredible during the delivery and she is doing well now. Auggie is everything we could have hoped for and we can’t wait for you to meet him.
