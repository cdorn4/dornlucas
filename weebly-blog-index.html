Year End Review

12/10/2025

0 Comments

 
Another great year comes to a close. We were fortunate to do a lot of traveling in 2025. It started with the yearly trip down to Florida to visit Karen and Al. Every year, Auggie gets more adventurous with his swimming. That was quickly followed by a spring break trip to Copenhagen. We met up with Julia, Jeff and Egan and spent the week eating our way through town. Highlights include a Go Boat ride amongst the canals, meals at a couple Michelin star restaurants, and renting bikes to explore the city. Once school got out, we were on our way to Alaska for a National Geographic cruise with Karen. Everything about it was incredible - the people, food, unique experiences and obviously the views. We traveled all over South East Alaska and then finished up the trip in Glacier Bay. See July's post for a full run down. The last big trip of the year was out to the East coast. We flew into Jersey, rented a car and hit up all the New England states before visiting Bill and Jenny in Maine. It's always so peaceful visiting them, and it meant a lot to be able to bring Auggie. We then went down to a rented house in Woodstock, NY and had a great time connecting with Huck and Justine. In between all of this we went camping a number of times in Two Rivers, and had a great Lucas family get-together up at the cabin in Moose Lake. 

On the personal side, Auggie is doing a great job of being 4. He started 4K in the Fall, and is keeping busy with Karate and Piano. He just earned his yellow belt, and is sight reading Rudolf the Red Nosed Reindeer! This was the year that he started really enjoying playing with Lego's, which makes Chris incredibly happy. Elyse is doing an incredible job creating community projects with her students. She has had student work shown at the Symphony, Performing Arts Center and the new public Library. She's even been on the news a couple of times! Chris is doing more art then normal. He's involved with a local drawing club that does figure drawing every other week, and has started shooting film again. Dewy is alive and more groany and cuddly than ever. 

We wish you all good health and many adventures during this next year!

Alaska!

7/11/2025

0 Comments

 
We took an unbelievable Alaskan vacation to celebrate Chris's Moms 75th Birthday. The adventure started in Ketchikan where we boarded the National Geographic Sea Lion ship. There were only 60 people and a half dozen naturalists aboard. The first day we stopped in God's Pocket and saw brown bear, whales and took a zodiac cruise around the bay. The second day we cruised through the Wrangell Narrows and got our fill of eagles. We then stopped at the town of Wrangell where Auggie got many Garnets and we explored the petroglyphs on the beach. The next day we went through the Sumner Strait and stopped to hike in the luscious rain forest and kayak in the bay. Day 4 was defiantly our favorite. We sailed into Tracey Arm and took a zodiac ride amongst the icebergs. It was otherworldly. The last day of the cruise we got off in Juneau only to get on a plane to Glacier Bay. We spent 3 days at the Glacier Bay lodge. The full day we were there we took an all day boat ride to see the Glaciers. On the way we say dozens of humpback whales, brown bear and an orca! Along the shore there were countless sea otter - so cute! 
Chris took a 35mm camera on the trip. The location made taking photographs especially easy.

Year End Review

12/11/2024

0 Comments

 
2025 is here! That means 5 years since covid, 5 years since cancer?! 2024 was a fantastic year! Elyse was named one of Fox Cities Chamber of Commerce outstanding teachers. The night of the award ceremony she was announced as the Teacher of the Year! So proud and so well deserved. We spent spring break down in Florida, and Auggie got his swimming flippers. Auggie celebrated his third birthday with an 'I dig being three' construction themed party. 

Summer was a whirlwind of travel. Chris and Elyse chaperoned Elyse's high school students on a trip of Italy while Auggie stayed home with Nana and Grandpa John. The trip was relatively drama free, although not a way of travel that we like (tour busses, guides, new city every day? gross). Three days after getting home we were in Michigan for Friendscation. It's so much fun that all the kids can play together now. We threw a surprise 75th party for John's birthday. Everyone was in town because that weekend was the Pink Party - a celebration of 5 years cancer free. We booked Bjorklunden for the weekend and had a great time with friends and family in Door County. A couple of weeks later we were on a train from Chicago to Glacier National Park. Auggie enjoyed the train, but agreed it was 12 hours too long. Our new country of the year for Auggie was Canada. We spent 4 days in Canmore going on day trips to Banff and the surrounding area. Then we were down at the Village Inn on Lake McDonald for a night before finishing the trip in an Airbnb just outside Glacier. Auggie did a great job hiking, rafting and enjoying the mountains. Back in the Midwest we went camping with Auggie a few times and he had a blast each time. Looking forward to more of that in the future. 

The Fall had us taking weekend trips to visit friends, enjoy the colors in Door County, and having people over for the Packers games. At Thanksgiving down at the Dorn's, we surprised Karen with a 75th Birthday party and video. We went on the Polar Express at the Railway Museum in Green Bay and went all the way to the North Pole! Or at least around the property 4-5 times. Elyse was just in New York to help Justine with the birth of Huck Quincy Lucas! Auggie is so excited to have another little cousin.  We're looking forward to spending Christmas down in North Carolina with Matt and the fam. 

We wish you all good health, yummy food and exciting adventures for 2025.

Out West

8/27/2024

0 Comments

 
Auggies new country for the year is Canada! Our trip began with the Empire Builder train from Chicago to Whitefish, Montana. We had our own sleeper car, and for the most part Auggie enjoyed the ride. We then drove up to Canada where we stayed in Canmore for a few days. Adventures included a rafting rip on the Bow River, a ride up the Banff Gondola, hiking in Banff, and high tea at the Prince of Wales Hotel. 
The second half of the trip was in and around Glacier National Park. Our first night we stayed at the Village Inn which is in the Park on the southern shore of Lake McDonald. It was an awesome place for Auggie to play in the water, climb on rocks, and take in the incredible view. The last three nights were spent in Columbia Falls in a little Airbnb out in the country. Our neighbors were some lovely horses, and the views from the porch were great. We went on a number of hikes in Glacier, saw lots of deer and a Moose, and went on a boat ride on St. Mary Lake. Overall it was a great trip. We didn't try to overdue it, instead we planned on activity each day and then let Auggie decide how he wanted to spend the rest of the day.

A lot of Summer in 3 weeks

7/5/2024

0 Comments

 
Five days after getting back from Italy we were on our way to Michigan for Friendcation. This yearly tradition is such a blast - especially as the kids are getting older and can be more independent. We spent the long weekend either on the beach, around a campfire or in a hot tub. 
School got out and three days later we're on a plane to chaperone 32 high school students in Italy. The itinerary was bonkers - fly into Milan, bus to Verona, two nights in Venice, then to Florence and San Gimignano, down to Assisi for a day, Pompeii and Sorrento for a night, over to Capri for the day and finally Rome. The kids were great, but we learned a lot about 'chaperoning'. Group travel isn't our preferred way to go but it was fun revisiting many places we had already been. Auggie went bowling for the first time at the same place Elyse and the fam would bowl when they were younger - a real trip. We met Chris's folks in Milwaukee for an overnight trip centered around the Zoo. It was our first time there and a great size with all the animals you'd want to see.
