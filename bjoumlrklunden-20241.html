ABOUT THE WEEKEND

Join us for a family-friendly pink weekend in the woods! Similar to our wedding, we have rented the entire Bjorklunden lodge and encourage everyone to stay there on site. 

Guests are welcome to check in Friday afternoon, and we will be meeting up at the Door County Brewing Co for food trucks and drinks. Saturday, we are asking everyone to think PINK and sport that color in honor of breast cancer awareness throughout the day. We will start with a low key 5-k run/walk followed by lunch, rose’ and an afternoon of pink-tivites. We'll be having a good old fashioned pig roast for dinner Saturday night along with dancing. Sunday, we will check out of the lodge in the morning and bid farewell to NE WI with a stop over at the Stabbur Beer Garden. 
​
We all have such busy lives and miss seeing each other, so what better reason to get everyone together than Elyse's 5 year cancerversary. We hope you can make it to help celebrate! 

Remission, Rose’ and Revelry! Huzzah!
