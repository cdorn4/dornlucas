Björklunden 2024

A pink weekend in the Door County woods celebrating Elyse’s 5-year cancerversary — Remission, Rosé, and Revelry!

Similar to our wedding, we rented the entire Björklunden lodge on the shores of Lake Michigan in Door County and invited our friends and family to stay together on site.

Throughout Saturday, everyone thought PINK and sported the color in celebration of Elyse’s 5 years cancer-free. The weekend kicked off at Door County Brewing Co with food trucks, followed by a 5K run/walk to Roost & Banter Cafe, rosé sampling, kids’ yard games, an evening BBQ feast, dancing, and lakeside bonfires, wrapping up with Sunday farewells at Stabbur Beer Garden.
