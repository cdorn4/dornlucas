It’s been such a warm fall that these outdoor activities have seemed more like spring than November. First we saw Santa at the holiday market! Auggie did a lengthy ramble on what he wanted, with lots of clarification from Mom and Dad. We went on a little sleigh ride and enjoyed many of the sweeter things of the holiday season.

The next weekend Karen and Gus were up and we all went to the Packers game. It was Auggies first time going so we did it up! Tailgating at 9am with beers and brats . . . and juice boxes and hot dogs. Auggie enjoyed the whole spectacle of it, the game time treats and cheering as loudly as you can. The one thing that wasn’t appreciated was the loud jet flyover. It was a hugely successful day, even if the Packers didn’t win.
