Well, a lot has happened, and at the same time not that much at all. Our honeymoon was scrapped, so we explored the beautiful Midwest with friends and family. We've refinished the floors in our guest room, baked sourdough, camped at a social distance and painted our front porch. Most of the summer was spent at the dock in Moose Lake. We said farewell to Aidan, who is going for his PhD at UC Berkeley. Having him in Appleton the past four years had been a real treat - it was sad to see him go.
