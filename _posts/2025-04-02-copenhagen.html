We went to Copenhagen for spring break! Turns out it's very cheap to fly to Copenhagen. And that means friends joined us in Copenhagen!! We spent a whole week in the city with Jeff, Egan and Julia. Our Airbnb was beautiful and centrally located to everything. Highlights include a 3 hour cruise on a GoBoat with frequent stops for drinks and provision, a date night at Boutique Emilia (thanks friends), a trip up to the Louisiana Museum of Modern Art, a bike day around the city, and an unforgettable meal at Barr. Every day was a new walking adventures to some corner of the city. Auggie officially got the Lego bug which makes Chris very happy. For the entire trip we always had beautiful sights, bellies full of pastries and good times being together. 
The Holga came with on the trip with moderate success.
