Summer keeps rolling at a fast pace. The weekend we got back from Alaska we were camping at Oakfest in Plymouth. It was a grand college reunion with Holy Sheboygan. Auggie enjoyed the farm and we enjoyed catching up with everyone and listening to some awesome music. 10 days later we were on a plane to NY, picked up a rental car, and drove to Maine. We made pit stops in all the New England states just to check them off the list. We spent a couple of days with Bill and Jenny at their amazing property. We explored their shoreline, took a huge ATV ride, picked some wild blueberries, and went on a boat ride around Acadia. 

After Maine we drove down to Woodstock NY where we met Justine, Huck, Leta and John. We had an incredible rental house that was originally part of the Byrdcliffe artists community. Chris had not yet met Huck so that was a treat. Most days we did a little hike or outing. There were black bear around the property, and a momma dear and her fawn would pass by everyday. When we first arrived Ben and Hilary drove up to visit. It was so good seeing them. There was a cute crafts fair where tintype photographer Courtney Robertson was taking photos. She took an awesome photo of Chris and Auggie.
