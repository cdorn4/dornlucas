Fall weather and colors were in fine form this past month. We enjoyed an adults only Octoberfest, although it didn't pack as many people in as last year. This was our second year fundraising for the Making Strides walk, thanks to everyone who donated and Leta, John, Tammy and Dylan for coming out to walk. Grandma and Grandpa came up for the pumpkin patch and we were joined by Asher, Mary Kate and Isaac.
