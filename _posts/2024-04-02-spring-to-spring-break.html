We spent a week down in Bonita Springs visiting Chris's folks for spring break. The weather was nearly perfect and we were in the pool at least once a day. Auggie was cautious at first, but by the end he was jumping in the water with Mom and Dad. We had a private movie screening of Up, and a great visit from Bill and Jenny. 
There's been something every weekend for the past couple of months. Whether it be play dates with our parents group or one of Auggies best friends at school Oliver, a visit from Frankie, a St. Patrick's day feast, a visit from Aidan and Izzy, or the early birth of Zoe, life has kept us on our toes. We threw Zoe a baby sprinkle and it was wonderful getting to meet her. The highlight of Spring has been Elyse receiving the Fox Cities Chamber of Commerce shinning star award along with 10 other teachers. At the award ceremony we learned that she was named the TEACHER OF THE YEAR! It was a huge surprise but couldn't go to anyone more deserving - Auggie and I are so proud of her!
