2026 had the pedal to the floor and we were along for the ride! We kicked off the year with our usual trip down to Florida to visit Chris’s folks. A theme for the year has been Auggie's continued confidence in the water which made the many pools of Bonita Springs all the more fun. We had a couple of snow filled months around the midwest before going to Portugal for spring break. We had an awesome group of friends come with us this year, and we split our time between Porto and the Douro river valley. Check out the full Portugal post for all the photos and deets. Auggie's actual birthday was in Portugal, and then we celebrated back home with a Lego Party.

The rest of spring was spent seeing friends, going on weekend trips around the midwest, and getting more and more comfortable on a bike (although not all the way there yet). Once school was out Elyse and I were off to Greece to chaperone a high school trip. This was our first time to Greece and we had a blast! The art history, the food and those sunny greek beaches had us sometimes forgetting we were supposed to be looking after kids. The full trip can be found here.

This was our first summer with a pool pass and we loved going as often as we could. We flew out to Portland for Molly and Luke’s wedding, and then hosted them back in the midwest. Elyse was able to celebrate Alexis and Jason’s wedding in San Francisco. The whole Lucas/Clark-Johnson family got together at the cabin for which is now a most important Summer tradition. It’s such a joy seeing Auggie play with his cousins. We snuck in a number of camping trips and visits to the Cottage on Lake Michigan. More recently Elyse went out to New York to welcome Justine’s second kid, Alice, into the world. We then put on our dancing shoes the next weekend for Brian and Kat’s wedding.

Auggie started Kindergarten this Fall and he’s thriving. The school is a couple of blocks from our house, and we feel affirmed in our choice of the community public school rather than a charter. He continues to take piano and karate lessons and still enjoys both. He competed in his first Karate tournament, and was awarded the Responsibility Award at school. Auggie loves to color, learn about new things and is getting better at reading every day. Chris started a new job at Johnson Financial Group and continues to work from home. He’s been busy with two art groups in the area, The Appleton Drawing Club and The Photo Opp. Nearly every week he’s at a figure drawing session, and often uses the community darkroom to enlarge photos. Elyse continues to expand the art department at North High School and is constantly getting her students' artwork out into the community.

We wish you all good health and many adventures during this next year!
