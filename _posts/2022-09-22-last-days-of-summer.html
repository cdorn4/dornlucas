Even as summer wound down we were taking a lots of weekend trips here and there. We spent a fun afternoon in Titletown running around and enjoying some Krolls burgers. We had lots of walks - with Auggie doing all the walking - and first bike rides. For my Birthday weekend we met up with Phil, David and Tiff at the Bristol Renaissance Fair. Elyse had never been, and the steady supply of beer and people watching may have convinced her to come back next year. 

No September would be complete without a trip to Hofacker's for some apple pickin. Auggie especially enjoyed it this year because he was able to walk around eating and apple the whole time. Elyse's High School Friend Max got married and the wedding was at his childhood house, which is 2 houses away from where Elyse grew up. It was very nostalgic for her, and I appreciated hearing all the stories that being there brought up for her. ​We are in the back to school routine but the rest of our fall weekends are booked up - eek.
