Right after we got back from Florida it was time to dig being 3!!! This was the second year in a row that Auggies birthday coincided with a snow storm. This time we didn't have to move his party, but it did mean him and Mom had a snow day! We celebrated with a big snow 3, bagels for breakfast, mac and cheese for lunch and  cheeseburgers for dinner. The weekend brought his construction themed party. As usual mom and dad has as much fun putting it all together as he did at the party. A definite highlight was 'wooden' boards the kids all got to nail into with golf tee's. It was so much fun having everyone over to celebrate.
