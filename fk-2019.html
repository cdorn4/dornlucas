Sayonara Surgery

7/9/2019

0 Comments

 
Things began to feel almost back to normal the weeks after Elyse's last chemo. We had a lovely weekend up in Minnesota. Elyse took a family road trip with Justine and Matt down to North Carolina for some family time. Her energy has been coming back and her skin is tan. 
The next step in the cancer battle was surgery, and with no surprise Elyse kicked its butt too. The tumor and 8 lymph nodes were removed, as well as her port (which is named Portia, and we were able to keep). The whole thing went exactly as planned, and now we just have to wait on the final lab results.
